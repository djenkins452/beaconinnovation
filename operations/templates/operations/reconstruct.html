Reconstruct your first days

This is a guided order of operations — it loads no data on its own. Enter your real records; anything unknown, mark clearly in the notes as an estimate.

Order of operations

  1. Create your products (one per distinct design, including internal items like bins/swatches), then add a print recipe per variant with expected grams + waste per unit. New product →
  2. Enter each spool at its FULL 1,000 g start (not today's remaining weight). Use Opening → spool and enter 1000 g; later prints bring it down.
    An unopened refill loaded onto a reusable spool is just material you already own — don't record it as a second purchase.
  3. Log each historical print in date order, oldest first. On the print form tick Historical (back-dated) and set the real date. Select the products + good units, the actual spools, and the filament used (recipe estimate, slicer total, or measured — labelled). Failed/cancelled prints: enter what was actually used, not the full plan. Log a print →
  4. For items already in your room, tick Immediately fulfill the good units as an internal order on the print form. That records the production and a separate internal order + fulfillment in one step — finished units go in and straight back out, filament is deducted once.
  5. Check the result: each spool's remaining grams = 1000 + adjustments − recorded consumption; finished goods reflect what you kept; the history shows every step, in order.

Everything here is immutable + idempotent: a double submit won't double-count, and a mistake is fixed by reversing the movement (the original is preserved), never by editing history.
